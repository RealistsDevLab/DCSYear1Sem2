# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Responsibilities
- **Respect Everyone**: Show empathy and kindness toward other people.
- **Inclusive Communication**: Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- **Respect Opinions**: Be mindful of others' opinions and experiences.

## Expected Behavior
- **Be Considerate**: Consider how your actions affect others.
- **Support Others**: Help resolve conflicts in constructive ways.
- **Be Open-Minded**: Encourage diverse perspectives and be open to learning from others.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- **Harassment**: Harassment of any kind will not be tolerated.
- **Discrimination**: Any form of discrimination will not be tolerated.
- **Intimidation**: Intimidation and bullying are unacceptable.

## Reporting Guidelines
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please report it by contacting another community member or directly reach out to our leadership team.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies both within community spaces and in public interactions when an individual is representing the community.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders, who will review and investigate the incidents.  

## Acknowledgment
This Code of Conduct was adapted from the Contributor Covenant and can be found at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/.
