# DCS Year 1 Semester 2 Wiki Home Page

## Course Overview
This course covers the fundamental concepts and practices in the field of DCS. Students will engage in a variety of projects to apply theoretical knowledge practically.

## Getting Started
1. **Installation Instructions**: Follow the detailed setup guide to get started with the required software and tools.
2. **Account Setup**: Ensure you have the necessary accounts created (GitHub, necessary platforms).
3. **Initial Configuration**: After installation, configure your development environment as per the guidelines.

## Study Materials Organization
- **Lecture Notes**: Organized by week and topic
- **Assignments**: Due dates and submission guidelines
- **Project Guides**: Templates and resources to help with projects

## How to Contribute
- **Fork the Repository**: Make a copy of the repository to your account
- **Create a Branch**: Use a feature branch for your changes
- **Submit Pull Requests**: Follow the contribution guidelines when you're ready to merge changes.

## FAQ

- **How do I ask questions?** 
  - Use GitHub Issues to post questions or concerns.
